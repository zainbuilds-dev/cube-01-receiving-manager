# 01 - Customer Letter

**Status:** Discovery draft only. No customer interview, endorsement, or deployment is represented here.

Dear receiving team,

A supplier delivery is the last moment when a seller or 3PL can reliably document what arrived and raise a well-supported discrepancy. This project explores a receiving workflow that collects purchase-order expectations and carton photographs, runs explicit checks for identity, quantity, damage, and variant, and keeps the observations and decision together in an evidence record.

The current build is an MVP, not a proven replacement for an operator's inspection. A result may be uncertain, and the workflow should make it possible to review the underlying evidence rather than treating a model output as proof. Before recommending operational use, we need to evaluate it on representative, held-out receiving cases and learn which errors operators consider unacceptable.

We would like to understand how your team handles discrepancies today, what evidence makes a supplier claim useful, and when an automated result should be sent for human review. No customer outcomes or accuracy claims are available yet.

Sincerely,

Receiving Manager project team
